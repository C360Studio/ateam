## Why

Reuse existing Go execution, scheduling, accounting, grading, bundle snapshots and replay. Addresses #16; follows #58 / PR #77 and continues draft PR #85.

Preserve raw Mercury and Gemini evidence; publish only reviewed summary information. New live execution requires explicit approval of the protocol and limits.
